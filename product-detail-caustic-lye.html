⚠️ Handling, Storage & Safety

🛡️ PPE Required

  • Chemical-resistant gloves (neoprene/nitrile)
  • Safety goggles / face shield
  • Acid/alkali resistant apron
  • Chemical-resistant boots

🏪 Storage Guidelines

  • Use HDPE or SS 316L containers only
  • Keep away from acids and moisture
  • Ensure adequate ventilation
